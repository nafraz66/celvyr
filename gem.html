Gemstone details

Every Celvyr stone is natural and sourced in Sri Lanka. Before you commit, we'll send you real photos and video in daylight, share any treatment honestly, and arrange an independent lab report where available.

Delivery & returns

Fully insured, tracked delivery — island-wide in Sri Lanka and worldwide on request. We confirm the exact cost for your destination before you pay. If the stone doesn't match what we described, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we'll make it right.

Turning it into jewellery

Have a ring, pendant or pair of earrings in mind? Ask us and we'll suggest settings and trusted jewellers who'll do the stone justice.
